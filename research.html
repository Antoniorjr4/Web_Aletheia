Coverage and limitations

What changed

Rev7 introduces host-derived inventory and telemetry, and contextual scaling constraints. The Python baseline also received operational context, so changes are not attributable solely to the Aletheia compiler.

What did not happen

In the live scenario 05, the model chose Restart rather than an unsafe Scale. Policy rejection of Scale(api,64) was verified in the scripted test, not exercised by that live decision. N=3 is exploratory.

The axiom audit

Reported audited dependencies: propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice. Formal claims are tested with lake build --wfail.

What is not yet proved

A complete Rust/Lean refinement is not established. The linear capability model of Lean differs from reusable tool capabilities in the ARX runtime. Nested sagas and full typing of await/atomic remain outside T6’s scope.
